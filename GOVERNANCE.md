# Project governance (interim)

## Maintainer and scope
MJ Watz (`mjwatzmedia`) is the founder and current maintainer of the official AI for Advocacy repository. The official project aims to make civic information and advocacy planning understandable, source-grounded, user-controlled, and adaptable to different jurisdictions.

## Decision-making
- Anyone with repository access may propose ideas, documentation, tests, or code via issues and pull requests.
- The maintainer reviews scope, safety, product direction, compatibility, and acceptance.
- Only authorized maintainers merge into the official default branch or approve deployment, access changes, provider selection, budget commitments, and handling of user data.
- A proposed change is not approved merely because it was submitted or CI passed.
- Contributors should disclose conflicts relevant to a proposed source, jurisdiction, vendor, or civic-policy recommendation.

## Review priorities
Accuracy and source traceability; privacy and safeguarding; accessibility; minimal user burden; transparent uncertainty; regional portability; test coverage; cost and maintainability.

## Neutrality and agency
The software should support lawful participation across viewpoints without choosing a political position for a user. Users control their goals, edits, exports, and external actions. The product must not present allegations as verified facts or invented procedures as official guidance.

## Open-source transition
The repository is currently private and its final license, contribution certification process, trademark policy, and public-release timing remain undecided. This is an interim governance document, not an open-source license or promise of public release. Future governance changes should be reviewed openly with collaborators when appropriate.
