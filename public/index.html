YOUR FIRST CONCRETE STEP

Find the original document.

Look for the notice, decision, policy, or proposal behind your concern. Record who published it, what it says, and any stated dates. If you cannot find it, start by asking the institution where it is published.

Why this step? A primary source helps you check both the facts and the available process.
Before you act

Confirm who has authority, whether your chosen process applies, and any current deadlines. A draft from this tool is not a formal filing.

Before relying on a source

  • Does it apply in your location?
  • Is it an adopted rule or only a proposal?
  • Is there a newer or amended version?
  • What exact claim does it support?
