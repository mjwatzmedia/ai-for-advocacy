# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds, technical experience, civic interests, and perspectives.

## Expected conduct
Be respectful, constructive, patient, and evidence-oriented. Critique work and ideas rather than personal characteristics. Respect privacy, consent, accessibility needs, and the right to disagree. Keep discussions focused on product behavior, research quality, security, and community needs.

## Unacceptable conduct
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, publishing private case information, intimidation, retaliation, or deliberate misrepresentation of sources or other contributors.

## Scope
This applies to project repositories, issue and pull-request discussions, official project spaces, and events where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and response
Report concerns privately to the maintainer through an agreed private channel. Do not post sensitive details publicly. The maintainer may request changes, moderate discussions, restrict participation, or remove access, considering context, proportionality, and safety. If a report concerns the maintainer, a separate independent reporting pathway should be designated before broader community onboarding.

This interim policy should be reviewed with contributors as the community grows.
